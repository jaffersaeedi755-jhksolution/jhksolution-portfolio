01 / THE CHALLENGE

A failed payment was only
half the story.

The existing workflow identified failed payments and sent “Red Fail” notifications. What was missing was the recovery loop: checking those accounts again, recognizing completed payments, and updating customer status automatically.

02 / THE SOLUTION

Close the loop.
Keep every system in sync.

A scheduled workflow retrieves outstanding accounts, checks invoice status and remaining balances in Xero, then applies payment validation rules. When payment requirements are met, ClickSend sends a “Green Pass” confirmation and the customer’s status is updated.

  1. 01
    Track

    Store customer details, invoices, outstanding balances, and status timestamps.

  2. 02
    Verify

    Recheck pending accounts against the latest payment information in Xero.

  3. 03
    Notify

    Route the payment result to the appropriate customer notification.

  4. 04
    Update

    Move resolved accounts from Red Fail to Green Pass and record the resolution.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Less chasing.
Clearer payment status.

The delivered workflow reduces manual payment checks and administrative work, automates customer communication, and keeps payment recovery records current.
