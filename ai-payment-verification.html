INPUT

Xero Account Number mapping
OUTPUT
Payment status & invoice notifications
01 / THE CHALLENGE

Payment checks started with a manual search.

Staff needed a faster way to identify customers and retrieve invoice information from Xero. There was no dedicated vehicle licence plate field, and Australian and New Zealand operations required separate workflows. Outstanding invoices also needed a notification path.

02 / THE IMPLEMENTATION

Connect the conversation to the accounting record.

  1. 01
    Capture the request

    Dedicated Vapi assistants accept a vehicle licence plate through voice or text and initiate a payment-checking request.

  2. 02
    Find the customer

    Make.com receives the request through a webhook. A custom lookup uses Xero’s existing Account Number field to match the stored vehicle licence plate to a customer.

  3. 03
    Check invoices

    The workflow retrieves invoice and payment information, aggregates the result, and routes it to the appropriate payment-status response.

  4. 04
    Respond & notify

    The result returns to the AI assistant. Outstanding-invoice notifications are handled through the email automation path.

REGIONAL DESIGN & EXCEPTIONS

Separate operations. Consistent lookup logic.

LifeSaver Australia and Drug System NZ have dedicated assistants and Make.com scenarios. The workflows return whether a customer is behind on payments. When no matching contact is found, the assistant asks staff to check the supplied identifier instead of presenting an unverified payment result.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Fewer manual lookups. A direct answer for staff.

The delivered solution connects customer identification, invoice verification, AI responses, and email notifications. It reduces repetitive accounting searches and gives staff a more direct way to check payment status across both regional operations.
