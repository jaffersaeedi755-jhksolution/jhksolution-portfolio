01 / THE CHALLENGE

Service jobs needed a clearer reporting path.

A field service company managed its jobs in Workiz, while operational reporting depended on manually transferring information into spreadsheets. Tracking assignments, dispatch workload, and daily activity across teams made consistent, up-to-date reporting difficult.

02 / THE IMPLEMENTATION

Connect job events to structured reporting.

  1. 01
    Receive the job event

    A custom webhook starts the Make.com scenario when an event arrives. The supplied implementation shows the transition from scheduled job searches to a webhook-driven workflow.

  2. 02
    Retrieve the Workiz record

    The Workiz Get a Job module retrieves the relevant job details for the reporting pipeline.

  3. 03
    Map the operational fields

    Job ID, customer information, service address, dispatcher, scheduled times, job category, and assigned technician are organized into consistent reporting columns.

  4. 04
    Add the reporting row

    Google Sheets receives the structured record through an Add a Row step, including a timestamp for when Make.com sent the job data.

A SHARED OPERATIONAL VIEW

Give dispatch and technician reporting a common source.

The reporting structure supports daily job reviews, assignment tracking, and dispatch workload analysis. The project documentation also describes technician performance, job volume, and revenue reporting as part of the broader reporting scope.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Less manual transfer. More usable job data.

The integration creates a repeatable path from Workiz records to organized reporting rows. It reduces manual copying and gives the team a foundation for expanding its operational reports.
