01 / THE CHALLENGE

One journey, spread across disconnected systems.

Applications, patient information, clinical reviews, prescription documents, and storefront access needed coordinated administration. Teams needed a clear record of each application’s progress, with a way to request missing information and carry review decisions into the next stage.

02 / THE IMPLEMENTATION

Connect each handoff to the next action.

  1. 01
    Capture & organize applications

    Zoho Forms sends application information into dedicated OTC and prescription request modules in Zoho CRM. Creation workflows initialize statuses, dates, and related customer fields.

  2. 02
    Support clinician review

    CRM Blueprints structure approval, decline, and further-information paths. Doctors and pharmacy teams make the review decisions; portal permissions and notifications support their work.

  3. 03
    Generate & sign documents

    After approval for signature, a Deluge function connects CRM data with a Zoho Writer template and Zoho Sign. A completion callback matches the signing request to its CRM record, marks it signed, and triggers the next workflow.

  4. 04
    Update Shopify access

    Approved application statuses connect to Shopify customer management. OTC and Open Prescription tags control the configured product visibility and purchasing permissions.

FOLLOW-UPS & COMMUNICATION

Keep incomplete applications moving.

Prefilled forms reduce repeated data entry when more information is needed. Email templates, internal alerts, and SMS integration support updates throughout review, approval, and signing. Returned information feeds back into the review process.

03 / THE OUTCOME

A connected process with fewer manual handoffs.

The delivered ecosystem links application tracking, human review decisions, document generation, signature status, and customer access. Teams can follow the workflow in CRM while automated updates and notifications reduce repetitive administration.
