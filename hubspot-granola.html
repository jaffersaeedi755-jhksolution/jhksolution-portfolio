01 / THE CHALLENGE

Scattered data. Disconnected meeting context.

Client data needed cleaning and standardization before migration. Contacts, companies, and deals needed proper associations, while deal information required careful manual review. The Granola connection was present, but meeting information was not reaching HubSpot correctly.

02 / THE IMPLEMENTATION

A clear structure for records and relationships.

  1. 01
    Clean & migrate

    Standardized source data and imported contacts, companies, and deals into HubSpot with appropriate associations.

  2. 02
    Organize the pipeline

    Manually created and configured more than 100 deal records, including stages, values, and relationships. Refined pipelines and CRM views for sales tracking.

  3. 03
    Connect meeting notes

    Troubleshot and configured Granola’s HubSpot integration so meeting content could be saved against relevant CRM records.

  4. 04
    Verify the workflow

    Tested the note-delivery workflow and CRM activity tracking. The supplied connection screenshot shows a successful delivery.

HOW THE INTEGRATION WORKS

Save a meeting. Keep its context.

After a meeting, a team member selects “Save to HubSpot” in Granola. The configured integration creates a note against the relevant contact, company, or deal and includes a link back to Granola. This delivered workflow includes a manual save step after each meeting.

03 / THE OUTCOME

A CRM that brings the whole relationship together.

The completed implementation provides cleaner customer records, organized sales opportunities, and a working meeting-note connection. The team can manage contacts, companies, deals, and meeting context in a centralized CRM with clearer sales visibility.
