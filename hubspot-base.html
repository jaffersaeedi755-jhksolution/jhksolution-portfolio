01 / THE CHALLENGE

A complex journey needed a clear structure.

Base needed to manage applications, qualification paths, meetings, and memberships across multiple tools. Manual follow-ups and disconnected data made it harder to keep customer interactions and lifecycle stages aligned.

02 / THE IMPLEMENTATION

Build the foundation. Connect the journey.

Configured HubSpot properties, object relationships, deal pipelines, lifecycle automation, and user access settings. Then connected the CRM to communication, scheduling, payment, and data synchronization tools.

  1. 01
    Capture & qualify

    Application intake, contact and deal relationships, qualification logic, and referral handling.

  2. 02
    Book & communicate

    Meeting scheduling, personalized emails, confirmations, reminders, and Quo SMS.

  3. 03
    Route & recover

    Post-call decisions, cancellation and no-show recovery, and payment failure follow-ups.

  4. 04
    Nurture & convert

    Long-term engagement, deferred leads, membership conversion, and synchronized lifecycle records.

03 / THE OUTCOME

A centralized CRM with a clear next step.

The delivered system centralized customer data, reduced repetitive sales and marketing tasks, and improved visibility across the membership lifecycle. Automated communication and recovery workflows support a more consistent experience from application to conversion.
