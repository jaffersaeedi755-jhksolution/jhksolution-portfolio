01 / THE CHALLENGE

Lead capture, checkout, and delivery needed to stay in sync.

The business managed leads, purchases, email campaigns, and course access across separate platforms. Manual onboarding and fragmented customer tracking made it difficult to give buyers, non-buyers, and additional-offer customers the right follow-up.

02 / THE IMPLEMENTATION

Build a connected journey around customer status.

  1. 01
    Capture & segment leads

    Perspective assessment funnels collect responses and contact information. Zapier routes new leads into Brevo, where lists and attributes organize the appropriate audience.

  2. 02
    Nurture with conditional logic

    Brevo sequences separate non-buyer nurturing from customer onboarding. Timed follow-ups check purchase status so a customer can exit the non-buyer path after converting.

  3. 03
    Synchronize purchase events

    Dedicated Zapier workflows connect ThriveCart product and bump purchases with Brevo contact updates. Customer status, audience lists, and purchase attributes drive the next communication.

  4. 04
    Deliver the purchased resources

    ThriveCart fulfillment maps purchases to ThriveCart Learn courses and bundles. A separate bump-purchase email provides the scheduling link for the additional coaching session.

BUYERS, NON-BUYERS & ADDITIONAL OFFERS

Different actions lead to different messages.

Customer-status checks and the bump-purchase attribute connect the email journey to what a contact has purchased. This gives the team distinct onboarding, nurture, and additional-offer paths within a shared CRM structure.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Sales, marketing, and fulfillment in one connected process.

The delivered framework reduces manual transfers between funnel, checkout, CRM, and learning tools. It supports targeted communication and automated enrollment, with a reusable foundation for additional digital products.
